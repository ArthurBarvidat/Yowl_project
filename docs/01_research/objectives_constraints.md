### Objectives & Constraints

#### Objectives

##### Shared Posts & Content

- Limit the size and duration of a post
- Limit the length of messages
- Filter posts by category
- System for posting, likes, and comments

##### Global Discussion

- Limit the number of messages in the global discussion (moderation)
- Optimized system with various selection criteria

##### Global

- Quick setup
- Mobile-friendly interface
- Optimized app
- App deployment

#### Constraints

- Limited time
- Large database
- Moderation system difficult to implement
